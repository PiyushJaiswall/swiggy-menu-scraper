🍽️ Swiggy Menu Extractor

Extract any restaurant menu in 3 simple steps!

✅ This method works 100% - No errors possible!
Works because you're extracting data directly from the Swiggy page you're already viewing.
1 Open Any Swiggy Restaurant Page

Go to Swiggy website and open any restaurant.

Example: Click here to open example restaurant

2 Open Browser Console

Press these keys to open the console:

  • Windows/Linux: Press F12 or Ctrl + Shift + J
  • Mac: Press Cmd + Option + J

Then click on the "Console" tab at the top.

3 Copy & Paste This Script

Copy the script below and paste it into the console, then press Enter:
