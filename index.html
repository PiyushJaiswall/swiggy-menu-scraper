Swiggy Menu Downloader

This tool gives you a bookmarklet that works directly on Swiggy’s website.

  1. Make sure your browser’s Bookmarks Bar is visible (Ctrl+Shift+B).
  2. Drag the orange button below to your bookmarks bar.
  3. Open any Swiggy restaurant page (with menu visible).
  4. Reload the restaurant page once.
  5. Click the “Download Swiggy Menu” bookmark.
Download Swiggy Menu

When you click the bookmark on a restaurant page, it will:
✅ Read the existing menu request Swiggy already made
✅ Download a CSV file with all items
✅ Show a small menu overlay on the right side of the page
